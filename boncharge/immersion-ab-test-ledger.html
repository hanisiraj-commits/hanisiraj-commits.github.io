9 findings

Found by recomputing every table in the deck against itself. The partnership-code tables, all the Meta tables, the Memorial Day market table, both sales’ creative ROAS, the September forecast and the Peak 2026 targets all reconcile exactly — these are the exceptions.

Google quarter revenue does not reconcile between two tables

By campaign type the quarter is A$2,796,281 on A$307,348 (9.10×). By region it is A$3,244,719 on A$307,353 (10.56×). Same spend, A$448,438 more revenue. Every row inside each table is internally correct, so one of the two totals is wrong.

Section 6 and forecast

Definition mismatches

read as errors, may not be

Internally consistent, but inconsistent with each other — so a reader who checks the maths will conclude the deck is wrong.

·

The May–July AOV column is not revenue divided by orders

$8,732,037 ÷ 28,517 = $306, not $287. Consistently about 6.5% lower across every month, so it is a different revenue basis — but the Q4 2025, Memorial Day market and Q4 target tables all do use revenue divided by orders. So “a $333 basket against $287” compares two definitions.

·

Product shares and category shares use different bases

Categories are computed on the full $8,732,037; products on roughly $8,513,000. The Memorial-versus-Mid-Year product table uses the full base correctly.

·

“Signups +27.9%” is a rate lift, not a count lift

1,899 against 1,462 is +29.9%; the rate lift 8.54% against 6.67% is +27.9%. Consistent across every card — worth labelling as rate.
