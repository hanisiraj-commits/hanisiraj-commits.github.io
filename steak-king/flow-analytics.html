Flow and campaign revenue are Klaviyo-attributed conversion value against the Shopify Placed Order metric. % of Shopify revenue = flow revenue ÷ all order revenue. % of attributed = flow revenue ÷ (flow + campaign attributed). New vs repeat is reconstructed at order level (first-ever order per customer = new). New-subscriber counts come from the Subscribed to Email Marketing metric; net growth subtracts Unsubscribed from Email Marketing. Bulk list imports are separated out of the acquisition trend and excluded from revenue-per-subscriber and correlations. Current month is month-to-date, hatched. Rates computed on delivered. Attribution windows apply — treat cross-channel comparisons directionally.
