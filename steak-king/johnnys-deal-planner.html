Moving a deal: grab the handle on the left of any row and drag it up or down the table. Everything in between shifts along by one to close the gap — drag Tuesday's deal down to Friday and Wednesday, Thursday and Friday each move up a day. A deal pushed past the last available day drops into Not scheduled at the bottom, and can be dragged back in at any time. Moves are shared with everyone on this page within fifteen seconds, and sign-off and comments travel with the deal, not the date — approve something on the 5th, move it to the 12th, and it is still approved.

Hero images: each deal ships with its PDP hero already built — the real product photograph with the value laid over it, the deal price against the everyday price and what you save. Copy it to the clipboard or download it straight from the deal view. They are generated from the plan itself, so the figures on a badge can never disagree with the figures on the page: run python3 scripts/build-deal-badges.py after a price changes and the affected heroes rebuild. Headlines and the corner tab live in scripts/badge-copy.json.

How to add a month: open this file and append one entry to the PLAN object at the top of the script — a label, the block rows, and one entry per day, each with its own id. Nothing else needs touching; the calendar, lookahead and build specs all read from it. The dates in PLAN are only the starting order — once anyone drags a row, the running order is whatever the shared backend says it is.

Keyboard: ← → move a day · T jump to today · tab to a drag handle and use ↑ ↓ to move a deal without dragging. Every date is linkable — the URL updates as you click, so you can paste someone straight onto a specific day.
