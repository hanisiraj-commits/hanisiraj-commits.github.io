  • The character is fixed. It is the approved Back to School cutout, Johnny with the tomahawk in the schoolboy uniform. Do not regenerate, re-cut or replace it.
  • Fixed furniture: red and white scalloped awning, black MEAT EMPORIUM SALE banner with 優質凍肉專門店, red BACK TO SCHOOL EDITION tab, ruled notebook page with spiral binding and pink margin rule, starburst badge, wood counter, red paper-plane doodle.

  • Copy shows the dollar drop, never the percentage. Percentages ride the starburst sticker only. Striploin is 64% off, salmon is 73% off.
