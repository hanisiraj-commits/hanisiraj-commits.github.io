01Objectives

Two jobs, one build

Objective 1 — close ten seats by 31 August

Working back from a 15 to 20% application-to-enrol rate, the target is 55 to 70 applications inside 25 days. Every channel below is sized against that number, not against a spend figure.

Objective 2 — leave a channel running

Everything built for the sprint stays live for Cohorts 02 and 03: the landing page, the pixel, the applicant list, the lookalike seeds and the waitlist. Nothing here is disposable.

02The demand picture

The category has no search demand

This is the finding that sets the entire allocation, so it is worth being precise about.

Query expansion on education fellowship india returns teacher fellowships, teaching fellowships and scholarships. Google's model does not recognise "a fellowship for people who want to build a school" as a thing that exists. There is no category-level search demand to capture, at any budget.

Search can only harvest demand that is already being expressed. For a product the market has no name for yet, that puts a hard ceiling on what search can contribute, and it will not lift with more spend.

What does exist is demand for the job

how to start a school in india carries a deep state-level tail across Karnataka, Kerala, Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Telangana and Delhi, which is a reliable signal of sustained volume. That searcher is looking for a licence, a compliance checklist or a franchise. They are reachable, but they arrive with the wrong frame and the landing page has to do the reframing.

Practical consequence: search is a capture channel here, not an acquisition channel. It is sized at ₹15,000 for the sprint and roughly two seats. The volume has to come from targeting people by who they are, which is what LinkedIn does and search cannot.

04Channel plan

LinkedIn carries it. Meta retargets. Search captures.

Allocation follows the demand picture. Where we cannot buy the query, we buy the person.

Primary · ₹55,000

LinkedIn Ads

The only channel where the exact person can be bought directly rather than found by an algorithm. Education is LinkedIn's cheapest vertical globally at roughly $4.18 CPC, and India CPCs sit well below the platform average.

  • Audience A, titles. Principal, Vice Principal, School Director, Head of School, Academic Director, Academic Head, Director of Curriculum, Founder, Co-Founder. Geo: Mumbai, Bangalore, Delhi NCR, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai.
  • Audience B, skills and groups. Curriculum Development, Educational Leadership, Instructional Design, School Management, plus members of India education groups. Industry filter: Education Administration, Primary and Secondary Education, E-Learning.
  • Audience C, matched. Upload alumni from prior cohorts, the March mixer list and past applicants. Layer a lookalike on top once the list is seeded.
  • Formats. Single image and a Document ad carrying a genuine "what building a school actually takes" asset as the lead magnet. Conversation ads reserved for the matched list only.
  • Objective. Native lead-gen form for cold, website conversion for warm. Manual CPC bidding, opened at the floor.

Expected: ₹1,500 to ₹3,000 per application, so 18 to 36 applications.

Secondary · ₹25,000

Meta — retargeting only

No cold prospecting and no creative testing in this window. Meta needs roughly 50 conversions per ad set per week to exit the learning phase, and this cohort offers ten. Pointed at warm pools it needs none of that, because the audience is already qualified.

  • Pools. Site visitors 180 days, Instagram engagers 365, Facebook engagers 365, video viewers at 50% 365, lead-form openers, and the uploaded alumni, mixer and applicant lists.
  • Creative. Cuts from the existing reels, founder-to-camera, Demo Day footage and alumni outcomes. Nothing new needs producing.
  • Objective. Leads. Never Purchase, there is not enough volume for it to optimise.
  • Frequency cap at 3 per week. These pools are small and will fatigue.

Expected: ₹800 to ₹1,500 per application, so 15 to 25 applications.

Capture · ₹15,000

Google Search

Sized to the inventory, not to ambition. The addressable pool yields roughly 250 to 350 clicks across the 25 days at 60 to 70% impression share, and there is no way to buy more of it.

  • Two campaigns. Brand, uncapped and always on, because everyone who reads the LinkedIn post and then googles the name lands here. Non-brand, capped, running the exact and phrase list above.
  • Manual CPC. Smart Bidding needs roughly 30 conversions per 30 days to function. Performance Max is off entirely.
  • Landing page must reframe. Search arrivals expect licensing and compliance. The page has to meet that and then move them, or the clicks are wasted.

Expected: 8 to 12 applications, of which brand will be the cheapest by a wide margin.

06Measurement

We optimise on applications, not enrolments

Ten enrolments is too little signal for any platform to learn from. Every bid strategy and every optimisation event points at the application.

Tracked

  • Application start and application submit, as separate events
  • Cost per application, by channel and by audience
  • Application to call-booked rate
  • Info session registration and attendance

Prerequisite

Applications currently run through a Google Form linked in LinkedIn comments. That cannot be tracked, cannot be retargeted and cannot be message-matched. A landing page with a tracked submit and a thank-you URL is deliverable one, and it is what makes Cohorts 02 and 03 cheaper than this one.

07Cohorts 02 and 03

Keeping the channel open at ₹35,000 a month

From September the account stays live between cohorts. This is what makes the second cohort cost less than the first.

The waitlist is what changes the economics

Between cohorts, paid points at a waitlist instead of a deadline. Anyone who does not convert for September is not lost, they roll to the next intake. A single lead gets three or four cohort attempts, so effective cost per seat falls with every cycle even when cost per application stays flat.

By Cohort 03 the account holds a seasoned pixel, a real applicant list, working lookalike seeds and a nurture pool. That is the asset this sprint is actually building.
