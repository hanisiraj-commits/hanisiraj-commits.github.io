Creative direction

The feeling we're after: dark, calm and premium, with light and water as the hero. Black Friday should feel like a moment, not a sale flyer.

The three frames below are mood images to start from, not the KV. You own the final scene, the angle and the device. Take what works, mix them, or go somewhere better.

A. The light column

One narrow beam of light between black and white Nano, catching both pours. Both colourways in one frame, and the light reads as the moment.

B. The glow

A dark kitchen at night, lit only by the panel and the stream. Strong at small sizes, like the inbox and stories.

C. The ripple

A single drop and the rings it makes, a nod to the name. Close and quiet, and it could work as a band or second image.

Do

  • Show the machine exactly as it is. Base and drip tray never detach, and the glass sits on the tray.
  • Show black and white Nano somewhere in the set.
  • Screen shows a believable temperature, e.g. 6°C cold.
  • Master Rippl logo only, added per email, not in the KV.

Don't

  • No type, price or "BLACK FRIDAY" in the image.
  • No black and gold, confetti or neon sale clichés.
  • No collage. One room, one light, one camera.
  • No AI-reshaped machine. Check the base at full size.
