The idea

This is not a funnel to the sample pack with the enquiry bolted on the end. It is a nurture with two doors held open the whole way.

The person on /pages/custom is one of two people. Either they have a brief and a budget and they are ready to talk, or they are sceptical and want to hold a printed can before they commit to 240 of them. We cannot tell which from a pop-up submission, and guessing wrong costs us the lead. So every email carries both doors, and their own behaviour tells us which one they were on.

What changes across the four emails is which door is primary. E1 through E3 lead with the sample pack, because it is the lower-commitment ask and the pop-up already promised it. E4 flips and leads with the enquiry, because by then the sceptic has had twelve days to claim a free pack and hasn't, which means they were never the sceptic.

Four jobs, in order: claim what you were just offered → these are the brands who already did → here is yours on a can → and it is smaller than you think.

Decisions on you

Four open items before Pass 2

  1. Hand-added leads. Outbound and event contacts land on the same list. As drafted, the trigger filters on b2b_source so only pop-up leads enter, and outbound gets a sibling flow on a slower cadence later. The alternative is one flow with a split at entry — Klaviyo branches can't rejoin, so that means duplicating all four emails into a second path for eight columns of near-identical copy. My call is to keep this one clean and build the outbound sibling separately, but it's your scope call.
  2. Sample pack URL. The pop-up brief sends screen 3 to /pages/custom-sample-pack. The URL verified as live at the last build is /products/custom-branded-sample-pack. Need the canonical one before any CTA gets written.
  3. Enquiry destination. Where does the second door actually point — the form on /pages/custom, or somewhere else — and does submitting it fire B2B Inquiry Submitted? That metric is the exit condition, so if the form doesn't write to it, people who convert keep getting nurtured.
  4. Shipping charge. $19.99 is inferred from the preview text on the live post-purchase flow. Worth confirming it's still current before it goes in copy.

Guardrails

Does not go in this flow

  • No percentage-off or discount framing anywhere. The pack is the offer, not a coupon.
  • No countdowns, no stock scarcity, no guaranteed delivery dates.
  • No client testimonial quotes. Logo wall brands are logos only.
  • Manufacturing timeframes are a guide, never a promise.
  • No em dashes in body copy, and no emoji inside the design.

Downstream

The live [WP] F_POST-PURCHASE_SAMPLE-PACK flow this hands into is carrying facts that are no longer true: "10 days from approval to delivery", "Priority Production. Limited capacity each month", "Your 60-day window closes in 7 days". Current spec is seven calendar days on priority and no monthly cut-off at all. It's a separate job, flagged here because it is the first thing a sample buyer reads after leaving this flow.
