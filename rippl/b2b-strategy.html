The five flows

There isn't a single B2B flow in the account today. These five cover the whole funnel. Timings are my proposal, open to your read.

Day 0
Who we are, and who prints with us. Straight to the logo wall. L'Oréal, Prada, Mercedes-Benz, Microsoft, David Jones.
Day 2
How it actually works. Configure, send us a logo, approve, delivered. Four steps and the minimum is 240 cans.
Day 6
Where the cans keep showing up. Boardrooms, black-tie, fashion week, hotel rooms. This angle is our best opener on record at 69.1%.
Day 12
Hold one before you commit. The sample pack, shipping only.

The campaigns

Three to four a month, so custom cans are already in mind when the need turns up. Most B2B buying comes down to timing.

Use cases, not deadlines. Our deadline-led subject opened at 52.9%. The use-case one hit 69.1%.
One idea per send. A new vessel, a turnaround, a brand that just printed with us.
Cut by deal state. Never sampled, sampled, ordered, lost. Each gets a different reason to open.
Revenue attached. The order event becomes the conversion metric, so we read revenue per send instead of guessing.
