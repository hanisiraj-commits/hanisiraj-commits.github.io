Personalised Reorder Flow

A traditional reorder reminder runs on a fixed schedule, the same day count for every account. We want to move away from that and send each account a reminder built on their own data: the average time they actually take between orders.

04

What we need from your end

The B2B order data reaches Klaviyo now, but only from 4 August 2026. To run this across every existing customer rather than only new ones, two things:

  1. Backfill the B2B Order Placed metric
    Every historical order pushed into Klaviyo, carrying its original order date. This is what the gaps are measured from.
  2. Send the average reorder time as a field
    On every account with two or more orders, mapped to b2b_avg_reorder_days. That single field is what the flow reads to work out the send date.
One fallback number, for accounts with a single order.
No thresholds or bands, just one figure until their second order replaces it.
