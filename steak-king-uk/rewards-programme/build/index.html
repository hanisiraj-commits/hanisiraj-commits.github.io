How it gets built on Rise

The workflows, the tier logic, where the customer signs in to see their credit, and what has to be confirmed before we start.

01Before we start

Two things to confirm

We checked the live store rather than assume. One finding changes the build, and one needs a straight answer from your side.

We could not find Rise on the store

There is no Rise script, app-proxy route or rewards page on steak-king.co.uk, and none on the HK store either. Rise may be installed in admin with nothing switched on, but nothing is live customer-facing today. Please confirm the account, the plan and who owns the login before we scope hours.

You are on Shopify Plus with the new customer accounts

Signing in at steak-king.co.uk/account redirects to Shopify's new passwordless accounts. There is no password. Customers either continue with Shop or receive a six-digit code by email. This is good for us, because it removes the biggest drop-off in any loyalty programme, but it changes where the wallet can live.

02The Rise build

Five workflows do the entire programme

Rise workflows are trigger, condition, action. Three issue the cashback at the right rate, two move people up a tier. Tier membership is held as a Shopify customer tag, which is what the cashback workflows read.

WorkflowTriggerConditionAction
Bronze cashbackOrder paidTag is sb-bronze, or no tier tagIssue store credit, 5% of order, expires in 45 days
Silver cashbackOrder paidTag is sb-silverIssue store credit, 7.5% of order, expires in 45 days
Gold cashbackOrder paidTag is sb-goldIssue store credit, 10% of order, expires in 45 days
Promote to SilverOrder paidCustomer total spent ≥ £250Add sb-silver, remove sb-bronze
Promote to GoldOrder paidCustomer total spent ≥ £600Add sb-gold, remove sb-silver
Account settings that matter as much as the workflows
SettingWhat we set it toWhy
Redemption priorityExpiring soonest firstWith credits stacking, the oldest must burn first or customers lose credit they could have spent.
Credit basisOrder total after discounts, excluding deliveryStops us paying cashback on the £7.90 delivery charge or on money we already discounted.
Expiry45 days from issue, per creditEach credit runs its own clock, which is what makes stacking work.
Code stackingCredit cannot combine with a percentage-off codeCloses the discount-stacking gap already flagged on the account.

One decision for you: when an order crosses a tier threshold, does that order earn at the old rate or the new one? We recommend the old rate, with the new tier applying from the next order, because it is simpler to explain and cannot be gamed.

The one build question on this journey

Screens 04 and 05 sit inside Shopify's new customer accounts, which are not part of your theme, so they can only be extended with a customer account UI extension. We need to confirm Rise ships one. If it does not, the wallet lives on a storefront rewards page instead and the account area links to it. Same information for the customer, slightly different build, and we would rather find out now than mid-build.

04Klaviyo

Rise feeds the reminder flows

The countdown emails are Klaviyo, not Rise. Rise syncs the wallet data across and Klaviyo does the sending, which keeps every message in one place with the rest of your programme.

What Rise sendsWhat we build on it
Credit issuedTriggers the day 0 confirmation, and starts the 45-day countdown sequence.
Credit redeemedExits the customer from the countdown so we stop chasing credit they have already spent.
Credit about to expireBacks the day 35 and day 43 messages, and the WhatsApp companions.
Balance and expiry on the profileSegmentation. Live balance in the copy, and the header chip on site.

Rise publishes the integration but not the exact event and property names. We will confirm those in the account on day one and map the five flows against what actually arrives, rather than building blind against a guess.

05Sequence

Four phases

Nothing is customer-facing until phase three, so the tier logic can be proven on real orders first.

PHASE 01Set upConfirm the Rise account, install and connect. Backfill tier tags across the existing customer base from lifetime spend.
PHASE 02Build and testFive workflows, expiry, redemption priority and stacking rules. Test orders through each tier before anything is switched on.
PHASE 03Surface itWallet, account tile, header chip and announcement bar. Klaviyo connected and the five reminder flows built.
PHASE 04LaunchAnnounce to the full list so every past customer starts with a live balance and a 45-day clock.

The backfill in phase one is the piece worth planning early. Every existing customer needs a tier tag before launch, or the whole list starts on Bronze regardless of what they have already spent with you.
