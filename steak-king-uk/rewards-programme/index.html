Bring them back organically

Give email and SMS real intent

04The platform

Rise.ai runs the wallet, Klaviyo runs the messages

Rise sets up a digital wallet linked to each customer's Shopify account. They sign in on the website and see a live count of the cashback they hold and when each credit expires. All of it feeds into Klaviyo, so every email and SMS can carry their live balance.

Rise walletIssues cashback on every order at the customer's tier rate. Holds each credit with its own 45-day expiry.

KlaviyoReceives every wallet event and the live balance on the customer profile.

Every messageFlows, campaigns and SMS show the customer their own balance, dynamically, on everything we send.
