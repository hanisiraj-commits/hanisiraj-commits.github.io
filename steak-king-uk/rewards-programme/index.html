Every order pays you back.
You have 45 days to spend it.

Customers earn a percentage of every order back as store credit. It sits in a wallet they log into, and it expires after 45 days.

Four steps. The customer only does two.

06The name

Four to choose from

It has to work in a subject line and sound like something a customer would happily repeat. The key visual above shows our pick.

Our pick

Royalties

“You've earned £7.43 in Royalties.”

A royalty is money you keep earning on something you already did, which is exactly the mechanic. Sits under Steak King without being a groan.

Tiers  Baron · Duke · King

The Cut

“Your cut of that order: £7.43.”

A cut is both a share of the money and a cut of meat. Short, confident, and it sounds like something a butcher would say.

Tiers  Trim · Prime · Reserve

Second Helpings

“£14.86 waiting for seconds. Until Friday.”

The warmest of the four and the most British. Sells the next meal rather than the money, which suits a food brand.

Tiers  Bronze · Silver · Gold

Steak Back

“You've earned £7.43 Steak Back.”

The plainest option. Explains the mechanic in two words with no education needed, at the cost of being the most obvious.

Tiers  Bronze · Silver · Gold
