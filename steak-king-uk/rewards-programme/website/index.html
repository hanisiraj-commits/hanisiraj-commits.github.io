Two things we need on the site

Steak Back is built and ready to switch on. The only piece left is giving customers somewhere to see their credit — and that has to be added inside your Shopify admin.

04/09/2026 · Both steps happen in Shopify admin

Steak Back pays customers store credit on every order, and each credit expires 45 days later. The Rise app is installed and the earning logic is live on our side. What does not exist yet is the wallet — the screen where a customer sees their balance and what it is worth. Everything below happens in Shopify, so it needs your team.

1

Add the customer wallet page

Rise adds this straight into your customer account area. Open the Rise dashboard, go to Assets → On Site, find Customer wallet page and press Add to Store. Rise walks you through the rest.

The preview above is Rise’s own demo shop. On Steak King it carries your branding and prices in pounds, and the gift card and referral sections are switched off.

WhereRise → Assets → On Site → Customer wallet page → Add to Store.
It then opens the Shopify editor at Checkout and customer accounts → Apps → Rise.ai. Save.
What it givesEvery customer a balance they can see, with each credit and its own expiry date.
Needs from usNothing. We set the Steak King colours and font in Rise beforehand, so it lands on brand.

Two settings to switch off while you are in there: hide the QR code (that is for scanning in a physical shop) and hide the refer-a-friend section until referrals launch.

2

Call it “Steak Back” in the account menu

The wallet needs a link customers can actually find, and it needs to use the name they already know from the emails.

WhereShopify admin → Content → Menus → Customer Account Main Menu → Add menu item → Apps → Wallet Page.
What it givesThe wallet, one click from the account screen.
Needs from usNothing.

Label it Steak Back, not Wallet. Nobody knows what a wallet is on a meat website — they know what Steak Back is, because that is what every email calls it.

Once those two are live we can switch earning on and run the launch send. Everything else — the reminders, the tiers, the artwork, the terms — is already done on our side.

Happy to jump on a call and walk your developer through it rather than doing it over email.
