What Steak Back looks like

Identity, tier badges and how the programme shows up in an email, on the site and in WhatsApp. Everything here is built from the crown already in the Steak King mark, so the programme reads as part of the brand rather than bolted onto it.

Concept artwork for internal review · final assets to be produced by the design team
01The mark

The crown is already ours, so the programme borrows it

Steak King is a king, the customer earns their way up, and the crown carries all of that without a new symbol to explain. The wordmark uses the same condensed display face as the rest of the brand.
