Steak Back email mockups

Built on the locked copy of 03/09. All seven on one template: hero, four data rows with no section heading, the sign in paragraph, the three tiers shown equally, the USP band, footer. Modules three, four and five are identical on every email and ship as saved universal blocks.

No mascot. Per Dave, 02/09, the UK Steak Back mark is objects only. The header carries the approved v6 wordmark, steak with coins beside it, light cut (steak-back-steak-coins-ink.png; the file suffix names the ground it sits on, not the lettering).

Colour calls: money figures use #C24A22 rather than #FF875F, which reads as the same coral but stays legible at text weight on paper. The primary button is near-black, because coral behind white text fails contrast. Coral stays on the kickers and the crown.

Blue, dotted{{ person.rise_wallet_code }}  A Klaviyo merge tag. Pulls live per recipient.
Amber, dashed[ TIER ]  A show/hide block on the tier segment, not a merge tag.
Everything else Static. Set once in the template.
