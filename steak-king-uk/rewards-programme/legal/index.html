Questions for legal

We are launching a rewards programme where customers earn store credit back on what they order. Eight questions before we switch it on.

03/09/2026 · Tethrd for Steak King UK
The questions
01

Does this need any FCA permission?

The credit can only ever be spent at Steak King. It cannot be cashed out, transferred or used anywhere else. Is that enough to keep it out of scope?

Electronic Money Regulations 2011
02

Can we advertise it as “up to 10% back”?

10% is only reachable once a customer has spent £600 with us. Everyone below that earns 5% or 7.5%. We show all three rates directly underneath the claim. Is that enough qualification?

CAP Code · DMCC Act 2024
03

Can we expire credit after 45 days?

Unspent credit disappears 45 days after it is issued. We say so on the rewards page, in the email that issues it, on the credit itself, and in three reminders before it goes. Is a 45-day expiry a fair term, and is that disclosure enough to make it stick?

Consumer Rights Act 2015
04

Do the expiry reminders count as marketing?

We send email and WhatsApp reminders before a credit runs out. They are useful to the customer, but they exist to bring in an order. Should we treat them as direct marketing and only send to people who have opted in?

PECR
05

Can we set someone’s reward rate from their order history?

The rate a customer earns is worked out from what they have spent with us over time. What lawful basis should we rely on, and what line do you want added to the privacy notice?

UK GDPR
06

What should the terms say about changing or ending it?

We may need to move the rates, the tiers or the 45-day window, or stop the programme altogether. Our intention is to honour any credit already issued. Can you draft that clause, and tell us what notice you want us to give?

07

What happens when someone returns an order they paid for with credit?

Our plan is to give the credit portion back as credit rather than cash. Do you agree, and should that credit keep its original expiry date or start a fresh 45 days?

08

What can we do if someone abuses it?

A customer can spend their credit and then refund the order that earned it in the first place. Can you draft a clause letting us suspend an account or cancel credit, and tell us where you want the line drawn?
