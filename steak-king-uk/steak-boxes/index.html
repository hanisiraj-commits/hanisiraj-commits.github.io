What changed against David's notes

  • Thickness. The fix was laying the steaks flat rather than standing them on edge — upright is what made 250g portions read as thick-cut blocks. Flat with the marbled face up shows a shallow 2cm edge, which is correct for the portion.
  • M9 marbling. Rebuilt around David's point that the M4-5 is already right. M9 is now one step richer than that and no further: red stays dominant at roughly two thirds of the cut face, with finer denser white threading instead of a pale lacy field. Applied to every image carrying M9, not just the two pinned, so one grade doesn't look like two products across the email.
  • Free chef's knife. Not an image change — the Steakhouse Duo row is missing the FREE CHEF'S KNIFE badge the other cards carry. That's a layout fix in Figma.
  • Files. Every image at its exact slot size plus an @2x. Portrait cards have no rounded corners or orange stroke — those stay on the Figma rect.
