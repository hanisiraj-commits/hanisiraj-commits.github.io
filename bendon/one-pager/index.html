01 · REPEAT PURCHASE, YEARS TWO TO FOUR

“Show the pattern for years two, three and four, and what the four-month window is worth.”

100 new customers place 162 orders and $165 of revenue over four years. Years two to four are 0.15, 0.12 and 0.11 orders each.

Your four-month finding holds. 45% of all second orders land inside four months, 70% inside twelve. But there is no bump at 18 or 24 months: the monthly chance of a second order falls from 6% to 0.7% and stays there. Nothing returns on its own, so the model does not bank a natural return — it has to be triggered.

The prize. Second order inside four months from 10% to 20%; inside twelve months from 18% to 32%.

02 · WHERE THE AOV INCREASE COMES FROM

“We cannot see how the AOV increase is derived.”
