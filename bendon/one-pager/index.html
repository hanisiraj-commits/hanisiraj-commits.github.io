02 · WHERE THE AOV INCREASE COMES FROM

“We cannot see how the AOV increase is derived.”

AOV = units × price × (1 − discount). You already raised price 31%. It went straight back out through a smaller basket and deeper discounting.

The +10% is half of that coming back. No price rise assumed.

One definition to fix. The “$92.90 today” is Australia’s FY26 average order in AUD; New Zealand’s is $77.05 NZD. We will state AOV per market and apply +10% to each. The $12m does not move materially.

“Re-examine the pot-of-gold assumption, the customers acquired and the ~$55 CPA.”

They are not lapsed subscribers. They are people who consented and have never been sent a single email — and there is no $55.
