What Papi's BF emails do

  • The offer is the hero. "20% OFF SITEWIDE!" or "BLACK FRIDAY 20% OFF" is the biggest thing in the email, set in heavy condensed caps.
  • The % sits on a red starburst. A jagged red explosion with a darker red drop shadow carries the saving. Smaller starbursts flag extras ("Limited Edition Christmas Jars").
  • Black bars with white type. Headlines and the code ("USE CODE: PAPIBF20") sit in solid black or red rectangles, like stickers slapped on.
  • Busy pattern backgrounds. Tiled cartoon pistachios or a wall of jars behind the hero, never a plain field.
  • Papi himself sells it. The moustache character appears in nearly every send, often with a script speech bubble ("Take 20% off sitewide, bella!").
  • Palette: olive green, black, Papi red and white. 2025 went darker: black with a torn white paint-stroke behind the type.
  • Code and dates on the hero. The code and the sale window (e.g. "26 NOV - 2 DEC") are in the first fold, usually as a red tab.
  • Christmas rides along. BF sends stack a festive extra underneath (Christmas jars, free Santa Papi ornament, Papittone).
