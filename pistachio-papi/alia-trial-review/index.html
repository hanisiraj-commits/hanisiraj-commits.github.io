01 — The advantage

Why it beats a standard
Klaviyo pop-up

Both pop-ups offered the same thing — 10% off a first order. What changed is what we are able to do with it. Klaviyo gives you one form, built from its own templates, shown on a fixed rule, the same to everyone. Alia removes all four of those limits.

Smart AI retriggering

Rather than firing once on a fixed delay and never again, Alia re-serves the pop-up when a visitor's behaviour says they are actually ready for it. Klaviyo shows it on a timer and gives up.

Designed properly, not assembled from templates

The pop-up is a fully custom design in the brand's own artwork and type. Klaviyo's builder caps what a form can look like, and every workaround costs us fidelity.

A/B testing at real speed

Six variants shipped inside the 30 days — mystery discount, mini quiz, scratch-off, removing the decline button. Klaviyo's form testing is slow enough that in practice the form sits untouched for months.

The success screen knows who it is talking to

New subscribers get the discount code. Existing customers get the same design with no code and no mention of one, so we never hand a first-order offer to someone who has already bought. Klaviyo shows one success screen to everybody.
