One ask: UTMs on Chunky bar sign-ups

We're sending paid traffic to the Chunky bar pages, and 135 pop-up sign-ups have come from those pages so far. Right now we can't tell which of them came from an ad, because the UTM parameters are dropped before the sign-up reaches Klaviyo. Of the 788 sign-ups so far, only 2 arrived with any tracking attached.

Could the pop-up pass these through with every sign-up on the Chunky bar product and collection pages?

  • utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content, utm_term from the landing URL
  • fbclid if it's there
  • Saved for the whole visit, so a sign-up two pages after landing still counts
  • Sent on the Papi Popup Capture event and saved to the profile

With that in place we can see which ad audiences bring in subscribers who go on to buy, and put spend behind the ones that do.
